A FEW THINGS TO KNOW / 008

Good to know.

How do I book a shoot?+

Send an enquiry using the contact form with a little information about your car, the type of images you have in mind and your preferred dates. We'll take it from there.

Where do shoots take place?+

Locations can be discussed when planning your shoot. Share your general area and any location ideas in your enquiry.

How much does a photoshoot cost?+

Pricing depends on the scope, location and requirements of each project. Get in touch for a quote before confirming a booking.

When will I receive my photographs?+

Delivery times will be confirmed when your shoot is arranged, based on the project and editing requirements.

Can I use the photographs on social media?+

Usage and sharing arrangements can be agreed as part of your booking. Please ask if you need images for a business or commercial purpose.
